# Art direction

Appended to every image prompt so a batch reads as one campaign. Matched to
lightschool.com: near-black ground, one huge line of white geometric type,
faint sacred geometry behind it. Edit and the next render picks it up.

---

BRAND: Light School. Match lightschool.com exactly.

GROUND. Near-black indigo, #050208 to #0A0510, and it must stay near-black.
Never navy, never royal blue, never a blue-dominant field — if the background
reads as "blue" rather than "almost black", it is wrong. Almost no mid-tones.
The frame should feel like a dark room with one source of light in it.

TYPOGRAPHY. The headline is the hero and must dominate the frame. Set it in a
heavy geometric sans-serif with near-circular bowls, a single-storey 'a', tight
letter spacing and tight leading — the Gilroy / Poppins / Montserrat Bold
family. Pure white #FFFFFF. Sentence case, exactly as the line is written — never ALL
CAPS, never small caps, never title case. Break it across two or three lines,
left-aligned, occupying roughly the upper half of the square. Type is crisp,
correctly kerned and perfectly legible at thumbnail size. No drop shadows, no
outlines, no faux-3D, no script fonts, no handwriting.

ACCENT. One treatment, the same every time so a batch reads as one campaign:
set the single most important word of the headline in warm gold #d4a037, and
underline that word with a thin gold stroke. Nothing else in the frame is gold
except the faint constellation lines. Never a filled pill, never a gold block
or button behind the word, never gold on more than one word.

BACKGROUND GEOMETRY. Barely perceptible concentric circles, like ripples
centred off-frame, under 6% opacity — if you notice them before the headline,
they are too strong. A few thin straight lines connecting small dots,
constellation-like, in one corner only. These sit far behind the type and must
never compete with it.

IMAGERY. One person, or at most two. Never a group, never a crowd around a
screen. Shot in a dark room lit by a single warm source — a desk lamp, a
monitor, a late window — so most of the frame stays in shadow. Low-key,
underexposed, deep blacks. Unposed and mid-work: nobody looks at the camera,
nobody smiles at the camera, nobody points at a laptop. Shallow depth of field.

The photograph is a fragment, not a picture: it occupies the lower third or one
side, is graded into the near-black ground, and dissolves into it at the edges.
No border, no white frame, no rounded card, no drop shadow, no collage. If it
reads as a stock photo dropped into a template, it is wrong.

COMPOSITION. Generous negative space. One idea per frame. A viewer reads the
headline first, the image second, the accent third, in that order.

TYPE DETAIL. The letterforms are geometric and WIDE, built on circles — a round
O, a round C, generous counters. Not condensed, not narrow, not a compressed
sports grotesque. Think Poppins Bold or Montserrat Bold, never Anton or
Impact.

NEVER: bright daylit offices, open-plan glass, people grinning at a monitor,
handshakes, whiteboard sessions, neon, cold blue technology light, sci-fi
chrome, circuit boards, glowing brains, robots, floating holograms, collages,
more than one accent colour, or lorem-ipsum filler text.
