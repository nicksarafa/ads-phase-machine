# Art direction

Appended to every image prompt so a batch reads as one campaign. Matched to
lightschool.com. Edit it and the next render picks it up, no restart. Untick it
and the built-in direction in `src/lib/brand.ts` is used instead.

---

Art direction: warm, premium, quietly cinematic, matched to lightschool.com.

Ground the frame in deep near-black indigo (#050208). The single accent is warm
gold (#d4a037), lifting to #e8c476 where light catches. Gold is the only colour
that leads the eye. Soft purple (#a78bfa) and cyan (#00bcd4) may appear far
back and dimmed, never together in one frame, never competing with the gold.

Real people at real desks, lit warmly and from one side, as if by lamplight or
late afternoon sun. Shallow depth of field, generous negative space, calm
composition. The feeling is a starlit reading room: unhurried, human, and
premium without being cold.

Light is the brand's metaphor, so let it glow rather than glare. Candlelight,
not neon.

Never: harsh neon, cold blue technology light, sci-fi chrome, circuit boards,
glowing brains, robots, stock-photo handshakes, or anything that reads as a
crypto ad. The subject is people making things, lit warmly.
