# Light School brand — the real one

Condensed from `DESIGN.md` in the lightschool-website repo, which is the
authoritative brand system for the live site.

**Read this before enabling it.** The art direction in `design.md` renders ads
in violet `#6868e8` on muted neutrals. The live brand is gold `#d4a037` on
near-black indigo — cosmic and warm, not cool and editorial. The two do not
match. Tick this file on and ads start looking like the website; leave it off
and they keep the current look. Both are one click, so try a cycle each way
before deciding.

Enabling this alongside `design.md` sends both, and the model will try to
reconcile two conflicting palettes. Pick one.

---

BRAND PALETTE — Light School

Ground everything on near-black indigo (#050208), the night sky behind the
whole brand. Primary accent is warm gold (#d4a037), lifting to #e8c476 for
highlights and deepening to #b8862e for gradient stops. Gold is the only
accent that carries a call to action.

Secondary accents, used sparingly and never together in one frame: soft purple
(#a78bfa), pink (#e06b8a), cyan (#00bcd4). Text on dark ground is pure white.

The mood is a starlit reading room: prismatic orbs drifting far behind quiet,
restrained type. Warm, hand-made, premium. Light is the metaphor — glow,
halo, shine — and it should feel like candlelight rather than neon.

Never: harsh neon, sci-fi chrome, circuit-board motifs, glowing brains,
robots, or cold blue technology clichés. The brand is about people making
things, lit warmly.
